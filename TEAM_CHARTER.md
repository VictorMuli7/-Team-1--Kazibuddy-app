SUMMARY OF THE APP
The Jua kali App is a web app that connects local artisans with customers looking for their services. It is for customers looking for artisans such as barbers, laundry cleaners, home and office cleaners, plumbing, cooking among other
service providers. The main idea behind it was giving a platform to the hands on workers to find employment and a living by enabling customers to find them.

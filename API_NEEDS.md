API NEEDS STATEMENTS
The organ donation app needs to read verified artisans and their services in order to identify workers who can provide services when the hospital needs them.
The organ donation app needs to read actions taken by the artisan (accepted or declined) in order to identify workers who have agreed to perform a particular service.
The organ donation app needs to read the prices charged by the artisan in order to compare services costs and select a suitable worker for the hospital.

REFLECTION PARAGRAPH
In our interview engagements, we came across a few challenges. The main one was how we were going to find common ground both as producers and consumers of APIs. As producers we engaged with our consumers, and we were guided by a single idea that led us to get out API need statements. The idea was that our app was to be a means of finding workers for their hospital app. In terms of our consumer engagement, we went into that interview with the same mindset of having an idea leading the producing team to create their API need statements.
